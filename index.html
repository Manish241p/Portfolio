FAQ

Questions clients ask.

Ask me anything
How long does a website take?

A typical business website takes 2 to 4 weeks. Bigger custom apps take longer. I give you an exact timeline in the quote.

How much does it cost?

It depends on pages and features, so I quote per project. After a free call you get a fixed price in writing, and it does not change unless you add work.

Will my website rank on Google?

I set up the technical SEO properly: structure, speed, meta, sitemap and schema. Rankings also depend on competition and content, so I never promise a fixed position. I do promise a solid foundation.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. I build so you can edit text, images and products without code, and I show you how.

What if something breaks after launch?

You get 30 days of free bug fixing. After that I offer simple monthly maintenance if you want it.

How do we communicate and pay?

WhatsApp, call or email, whatever suits you. Payment is split in milestones, so you never pay everything upfront.
